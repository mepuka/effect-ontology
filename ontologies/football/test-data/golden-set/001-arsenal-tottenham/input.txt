Arsenal secured a dominant 3-1 victory over Tottenham Hotspur in the North London Derby at the Emirates Stadium on Sunday afternoon.

The Gunners took the lead in the 14th minute when Bukayo Saka curled a magnificent shot into the top corner from the edge of the box. The England international has now scored in three consecutive Premier League matches.

Martin Odegaard doubled Arsenal's advantage just before halftime with a clinical finish after a brilliant team move involving Gabriel Martinelli and Declan Rice. The Norwegian captain has been in outstanding form this season.

Tottenham pulled one back early in the second half through Son Heung-min, who finished emphatically after a quick counter-attack led by James Maddison. The South Korean forward's goal gave Spurs hope of a comeback.

However, Arsenal's Gabriel Jesus sealed the victory in the 78th minute, heading home from a corner delivered by Saka. The Brazilian striker celebrated his first goal of the month in front of the home supporters.

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta praised his team's performance: "We controlled the game from start to finish. The intensity and quality were exactly what we wanted in a derby."

The result leaves Arsenal top of the Premier League table, three points ahead of Manchester City. Tottenham remain in fifth place after their third consecutive away defeat.

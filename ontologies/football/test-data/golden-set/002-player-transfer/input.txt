Manchester United have completed the signing of Dutch midfielder Frenkie de Jong from Barcelona for a fee of 85 million euros.

The 26-year-old has signed a five-year contract at Old Trafford and will wear the number 21 shirt. De Jong made 212 appearances for Barcelona since joining from Ajax in 2019.

United manager Erik ten Hag said: "Frenkie is an exceptional player. I know him well from our time together at Ajax and I'm delighted to work with him again."

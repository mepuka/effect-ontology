City Budget Update: Seattle Approves $8.5 Billion Operating Budget for 2025

SEATTLE - The Seattle City Council has approved the 2025 operating budget, allocating $8.5 billion for city services and infrastructure improvements. The budget was finalized after weeks of negotiations between the Mayor's Office and the City Council.

Policy Director Jane Doe presented the new budget framework during a public hearing at City Hall. "This budget reflects our commitment to addressing the city's most pressing needs while maintaining fiscal responsibility," Doe said.

The City Budget Office confirmed the allocation breakdown:
- Public Safety: $1.2 billion
- Transportation Infrastructure: $850 million
- Affordable Housing Programs: $600 million
- Parks and Recreation: $400 million
- Human Services: $350 million

The budget represents a 3.5% increase over the 2024 fiscal year. Key investments include expanding affordable housing stock and improving public transit options.

Jane Doe, who has served as Policy Director since February 2025, worked closely with department heads to prioritize spending. Her office in the Policy Office coordinated the budget review process.

The City Budget Office, led by Director Sarah Chen, will oversee implementation of the approved spending plan. Monthly progress reports will be presented to the City Council Finance Committee.

Mayor Tim Burgess praised the collaborative effort: "This budget positions Seattle to meet the challenges ahead while investing in our communities."

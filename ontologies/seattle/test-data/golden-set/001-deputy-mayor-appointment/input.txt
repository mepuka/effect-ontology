Mayor Tim Burgess announced today that Brian Surratt has been appointed as the new Deputy Mayor for the City of Seattle.

Surratt, who brings extensive experience in public administration, will begin serving in his new role effective January 15, 2025. In his capacity as Deputy Mayor, he will oversee day-to-day operations of the Mayor's Office and serve as the primary liaison between the executive branch and city departments.

"I am honored to welcome Brian Surratt to our leadership team," said Mayor Burgess at a press conference held at City Hall. "His background in municipal governance and commitment to public service make him an ideal choice for this critical position."

The Deputy Mayor position reports directly to Mayor Tim Burgess and is responsible for coordinating policy initiatives across city departments. Surratt will work closely with the Mayor's Office staff to implement the administration's priorities for 2025.

Brian Surratt previously served in various capacities within local government, bringing a wealth of experience to the role. His appointment comes as the city prepares to tackle key challenges including affordable housing, transportation infrastructure, and public safety.

The announcement was made during a staff meeting at the Mayor's Office, located in the Seattle Municipal Tower.
